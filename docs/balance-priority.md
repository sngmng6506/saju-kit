# Balance priority v1 — diagnostic only

## Source and frozen controls

[徐樂吾 子平真詮評注 論用神](https://sanqing.com.tw/en/%E5%AD%90%E5%B9%B3%E7%9C%9F%E8%A9%AE%E8%A9%95%E8%A8%BC/) explicitly separates day-master balance from month-command balance, climate, disease/remedy, special patterns and mediation. Four consecutive examples under 扶抑日元為用 were frozen at 76242a27e1397ebc7625ae6cc952a18434060ff0 before evaluation. Electronic witness only, not print authenticated. None of these four occurs in the prior 100-chart directional benchmark (not a claim of novelty against all 146 inventory entries). The source direction is supplied to the conditional branch, not predicted. Month-command and climate controls are recorded but excluded from scoring. The rule motivation also draws on [子平真詮評注 論用神成敗救應](https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_033.htm) and [滴天髓闡微 傷官](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/滴天髓闡微). General rules are provisional and not independent of the source tradition.

## Contract

`analyzeBalancePriority({saju,birthBasis,policy:'balance-priority-v1'})` preserves v1 balance candidates and fixed v4 direction. The new review layer examines only nominal category masses: weak branch compares output/wealth/officer, strong branch peer/resource. A category must be strictly largest in every standard AND neutral scenario, with identical scenario keys. Ties, zero mass or changing leaders abstain. Being the largest within a group does not establish actual excess, effective force or cause of weakness. The weak review mapping is output->resource, wealth->peer, officer->resource; strong peer->officer/output, resource->wealth/output. Strong review mechanisms are intentionally plural. Presence, unclashed substantial roots, and review relations remain separate observations, not vetoes. Absent element remains a hypothetical supplement. Neither reviewFocus nor its array order is a confirmed priority or ranking. All original candidates remain available. No new thresholds, weights, chart IDs or overrides. No useful element is selected and no effective interaction is asserted.

## Executed result

Actual 15-module V8 dependency graph of priority evidence, balance evidence, quantitative v3 and structure; unused throwing calendar stub. Both hypothetical branches of 100 existing charts (200 reviews): 72 multiple mechanisms, 68 unstable dominance, 44 single-mechanism review, 13 limited root evidence, 3 hypothetical supplement.

Four source controls (author's direction supplied):
| Case | Textual useful element | Review focus | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| BU-X01 | metal/resource | water/peer | excludes reference |
| BU-X02 | fire/peer | none | abstain |
| BU-X03 | water/officer | water and earth | includes reference, not unique |
| BU-X04 | wood/output | none | abstain |

Zero single-focus matches, one exclusion, two abstentions, one multi-focus inclusion. BU-X01 is the decisive counterexample to using wealth dominance alone to select peer: the author cites both wealth and officer pressure and chooses resource. BU-X02 invokes water formation and chooses peer; standard pressure leader changes between output and officer. BU-X03 chooses an unrooted visible officer; a blanket root-presence requirement would risk excluding this source choice. BU-X04 uses projected output, while support leader varies by season profile. These are textual controls, not scientific or statistical accuracy estimates.

## Decision

Reject deterministic dominance-to-useful-element selection. Keep reviewFocus as diagnostic only (not a product recommendation, ranking or selected element). Do not tune a rule on these four exposed cases. Next evaluate multiple burden categories and local resource accessibility, including whether the author uses a direct support mechanism or mediation; distinguish existing natal agent from hypothetical supplementation. Freeze new source examples before any policy migration. No change to existing strength or balance-v1 semantics.
