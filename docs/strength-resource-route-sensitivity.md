## Accounting

Three matched-scenario margins: (1) nominal peer + resource minus largest nominal burden; (2) peer + conditional direct resource minus largest of residual output and full wealth/officer; (3) same as (2) but subtract accepted officer-to-resource allocation from officer pressure. Officer allocation is bounded by original source budget and target capacity; it is never simultaneously added to resource. Wealth stays unchanged. All quantities hypothetical, not measured transfer. Keep exposure based on requested wealth, not capped wealth: a cap cannot erase overload. This diagnostic does not model multi-hop wealth->officer->resource flow or effective transformations.

H01: seated principal resource own 8.40–15.12, wealth request 21–28, officer request 13.75–15, officer accepted 3.076744–5.982734, conditional direct resource zero in all standard rows. Nominal full-support margin -52.78 to -26.73, route margin -61.18 to -41.85, officer relief unchanged because wealth dominates. Neutral route and relief margin -40.7 to -13.55. The existing resource model cannot explain the recorded strong reference. This does not prove that actual wealth destroys the resource; the rule is a hypothesis. H01 is an exposed secondary case with formation uncertainty; no label change.

W09 has same root range as H01 and two local anchored officer-resource-day chains, but none via a seated principal resource. Officer diversion narrows the standard route deficit from -53.73..-26 to -44.48122..-12.817368, without reversing dominance. XU14 has no qualifying anchored chain but small accepted officer allocations via less strictly anchored routes. Do not equate an allocation with a qualifying structural chain.

All 10 P1 candidate promotions remain pressure-dominant under route plus officer diversion in both seasons. Across 100 charts only H06 (weak reference) and XU11 (strong reference) lose uniform standard pressure under officer relief versus route-only. No standalone direction follows from this sensitivity.
