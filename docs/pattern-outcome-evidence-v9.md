# Pattern outcome evidence V9: purity and clarity

Opt-in `analyzePatternOutcomeV9` / `pattern-outcome-evidence-v9`. Source references: [子平真詮 論用神純雜](https://www.chenshupaipan.com/books/5/3880.html), [滴天髓 清濁](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/滴天髓/21). The first concerns compatible use relationships, the second broader coherence and interference. Neither is reduced to number of elements, gods or candidate families.

## Design

- Source purity checks five explicit day/month clauses, three aligned (wealth-officer, food-wealth, food-killing) and two opposed (hurting-officer, wealth-resource). Checks exact visible stems and directed element relation; candidate references are contextual. The Chen Ren case is storage context, not an invented hidden stem. Other readings not generalized. A match is an observed source subclause, not a final pure/mixed verdict.
- Candidate clarity separately projects V7 exact threats, valid rescue alternatives, interference, competing regulation and exception contacts, plus V8 assistant injury. Roles retain witness identity. A damage participant may be a victim; only obstacle roles contribute to dual helper/obstacle observation. Unclassified visible stems are not idle gods. No independent force summation or net score.
- Uncovered threat, missing-hour unknown and observed conditional rescue are distinct. Rescue never clears damage; absence of a covered threat never proves clarity. Both supportive and adverse evidence can coexist.
- V8 invoked once. No inherited fields changed beyond version/scope; no chart mutation, additional strength/climate evaluation, consumer pin or authority. Source purity and candidate clarity outputs are separate. Final clean/turbid, pure/mixed and clearing remain null.
