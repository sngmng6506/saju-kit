# Pattern outcome evidence v7: compound topology

Opt-in `analyzePatternOutcomeV7` / `pattern-outcome-evidence-v7`. Source: [論用神成敗救應](https://www.kaucim.ai/bazi/ziping-zhenquan/lun-yong-shen-cheng-bai-jiu-ying), [論用神因成得敗因敗得成](https://www.kaucim.ai/bazi/ziping-zhenquan/lun-yong-shen-yin-cheng-de-bai-yin-bai-de-cheng). These chapters distinguish support accompanied by adverse factors and rescue of adverse structures, and describe context-dependent reversals. V7 models necessary structural relations only; it does not conclude 成中帶忌, 因成得敗 or 因敗得成 as effective outcomes.

## Boundaries

- V6 is invoked once; its fields and reviews are unchanged except the new version/scope. `compoundReview` is separate, indexed by candidate ID. No re-evaluation of strength/climate, new selection, consumer or transit changes.
- `outcome-compound-topology.js` validates exact rule/witness identity and preserved threat roles. Duplicate or invalid references are reported; an invalid rescue does not count toward route coverage. Each damage witness retains its own alternatives, distinguishing route observed, no covered route and missing-hour unknown. Even full structural coverage does not establish actual rescue. Different witnesses, including multiple visible projections, are not merged by stem or summed as force.
- Support/damage pairs require a shared non-day participant; shared role alone is weaker than an explicit control/combination touching a support participant. A rescue may interfere with another support; V5 collateral officer control is explicitly retained. Shared helpers/combination participants are alternative evidence, not independent force. V4 conditional regulation is separately classified and can compete with damage on the same directed intervention. V6 exceptions link to the exact hurting/officer damage roles, without suppressing the threat.
- `outcome-compound-motifs.js` has six source-scoped conjunctions: officer wealth plus hurting; wealth officer plus secondary officer injury; killing food control plus resource obstruction; resource killing plus wealth damage; resource output plus wealth; resource excess regulation plus killing support. Join exact occurrence roles or explicit anchor bindings; never join just by family or same element. Conditions such as relative priority, excess, efficacy, and spatial interruption remain unresolved. Missing required rule means unknown, not negative. Other source reversals (including position-mediated output and blade food combining officer while retaining killing) need additional scoped evidence; no exhaustive coverage claim.
