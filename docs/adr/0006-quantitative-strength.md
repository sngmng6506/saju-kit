# ADR-0006: Evaluate root-aware numerical hypotheses separately

**Status:** Accepted as experimental diagnostics, not a production strength policy.

The prior static support mass underrepresents deep roots and seasonal conditions. Add a versioned ratio model with per-slot contributions, bounded root attenuation and alternative seasonal bases; enumerate fixed-day hour completions for missing hours. Preserve v1/v2 and structural v3. Explicitly do not integrate final strength: the measured development comparison has more incorrect numerical directions, and agreement-only coverage is low. See [contract and results](../strength-quantitative-v3.md). Native regression and new independent references remain gates.
