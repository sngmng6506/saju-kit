# ADR-0005: Evaluate structural strength hypotheses before changing quantitative policy

**Status:** Accepted for experimental research only.

## Context
Frozen ordinary v2 has systematic strong-reference failures. Root dynamics supplies richer evidence but cannot confirm actual interaction effects. Existing support index and final category gates may obscure qualitative differences.

## Decision
Add a separate opt-in qualitative v3 with traceable grounds and explicit conflict abstention. Keep previous APIs and metrics frozen. Reuse existing core and classification correspondences. Do not assign scores or infer actual protective/mediating effects, special patterns or favorable elements.

## Consequences
Allows direct comparison of qualitative policies without conflating numerical gate changes. Coverage and concordance must be reported together, including remaining false hypotheses. New unseen references and native regression are required before final hybrid policy or consumer release. See [contract](../strength-structural-v3.md).
