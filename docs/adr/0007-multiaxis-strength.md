# ADR-0007: Separate root capacity from support and pressure

**Status:** Experimental, not a final classifier.

Prior support-share models misclassify deeply rooted day masters. Preserve their exact per-slot contributions for environmental observations, use a separate positional root-capacity axis, and group potential protective paths by threat. Do not sum axes or infer actual interaction effects. Test explicit directional hypotheses separately; freeze positive and negative development comparisons. Independent reference evaluation and native tests remain gates. [Contract](../strength-multiaxis-v1.md).
