# ADR-0002: Scope ordinary strength separately from special patterns

**Status:** Accepted for experimental development, not production.

## Context
V1 frequently abstains when it detects a relationship or potential exceptional structure. Classical development examples expose limitations of its qualitative rules. The user chose to defer special-pattern interpretation.

## Decision
Preserve V1 and its regression baseline. Add neutral element-structure observations to core; add separate ordinary-strength V2 policy. Record directional relations and candidate mediation, without asserting actual transformation or transfer. Retain V1 quantitative assumptions to isolate the changed structural decision and gating behavior. Strong/weak degrees are not special patterns. Return caveats rather than automatically blocking every chart with a formation or clash. Keep unresolved or partial where evidence does not justify a candidate.

## Consequences
More useful conditional ordinary analysis but possible misclassification when real interaction effects or special patterns matter. The new policy is not a verified replacement. [Contract and gates](../strength-ordinary-v2.md) define the limitations. No consumer update before independent validation.
