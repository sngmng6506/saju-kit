# Pattern v6: officer/killing preservation guard

New explicit policy pattern-useful-element-v6. V5 remains frozen as a research baseline. Only preference changes; no authority/transit promotion. Source: [論正官](https://donglishuzhai.net/chapter/3744.html), [parallel witness](https://www.kaucim.ai/bazi/ziping-zhenquan/lun-zheng-guan). The Li case explicitly retains the monthly officer by combining killing. The guard applies to any day stem, not a stored chart identity.

When v5 prefers killing over a monthly officer, require all observed non-day visible killing stems to have an actual adjacent combination with a visible rob-wealth stem, without competing combination at either endpoint, no visible officer combination, no observed month clash and no competing month formation. These are conservative engineering screens, not sufficient classical efficacy conditions. A conditional officer-family hypothesis may be returned; otherwise abstain. No cancellation, transformation, purity, effective strength or 成格 conclusion is made. This does not resolve other ways to regulate killing, including food control or non-adjacent combinations. Do not use the same development case as independent validation.
