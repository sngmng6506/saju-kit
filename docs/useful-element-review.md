### Climate
Broad Xu seasonal cue only for winter metal/water (fire/warmth review) and summer wood/fire (water/heat-dryness review as an elemental diagnostic). Lack of cue is not lack of need. No physical temperature or moisture score. The source-specific profile is separate: 11 of 120 stem-month combinations transcribed from explicit passages in the electronic Qiong Tong source (winter Jia and Xin, summer Geng, noon/Wei Bing). Groups preserve the ordinary textual order or grouped requirements where contextual order is not resolved. These are seasonal-use passages, not universally pure climate prescriptions. Exact stems (including polarity), visible and hidden occurrence, role and clash exposure are separate from same-element evidence; no automatic substitution or reversal of order, no blanket precedence, no resolution of qualifiers. All other 109 combinations return null source profile. Source exception flags are review reminders, not executable predicates. No fallback inference by element/polarity. Future coverage expansion needs source evidence for each cell.

### Mediation
Five directed control pairs derived from elementRelation; unique intermediary from two successive generating relations. Actual routes require the same source and target as the original control edge, three distinct physical slots; mediator evidence includes non-day existence, anchoring, intact substantial root, local/one-gap edges and incoming controlling obstructions. Day stem may be a structural mediator but is never counted as an independent anchored mediation route. absentMediator means no non-day mediator node. No effect or successful removal of obstruction inferred. Nominal confrontation computes lower/higher mass ratio range and strict top-two counts for the pair over existing standard/neutral scenario grids, excluding day-stem mass under the existing kernel. Strict top two means both pair masses exceed every other category (no tie with outside category); it is ordinal, not a demonstration of equality, meaningful absolute strength, or actual conflict. Local-route-observed is existence of an adjacent independently rooted route under the ordinal screen, not a successful mediation finding. The negative controls retain actual routes.

### Disease/remedy
A control edge is a possible threat to a target, not a diagnosed disease. Another element that controls that threat is a remedy hypothesis; enumerate actual distinct-slot triple paths and potential incoming obstructions. Record whether the target is the day reference, month principal, same element as month or receives generation from the literal month principal. This does not choose which target is desirable. Do not count motif occurrences as votes, infer that all potential remedies are needed, or assert a disease. No special-pattern/medical meaning.

### Integration
Keep seasonal cue and source stem groups separate. Compare each against the inherited ordinary balance candidate set, alongside mediation pairs meeting the robust ordinal screen. A shared element is an overlap, not validation. An element outside the balance set is an opposite tendency, not a confirmed contradiction or unfavorable element. Remedy motif counts are contextual structural observations, not recommendations or scores. No votes, averaging, fixed method hierarchy, or automatic final selection; full methods remain available even when their comparison signals are absent. Existing balance priority and route review remain separately opt-in diagnostics, not additional votes.
