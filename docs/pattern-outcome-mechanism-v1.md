## Decision semantics

Supported / opposed / conflicting / unresolved refer only to explicit hypothesis conditions, not effective or ineffective actions. Directed control uses relative-force partial order, requiring source dominance for support; target dominance is opposing evidence, mixed axes conflict, equal or incomparable evidence is unresolved. Generation does not require source dominance. Root absence, nonlocality, shared contexts, pressure and combinations remain uncertainty, not failed force. Direct unexposed substantial root is a conservative sufficient observation for root-capacity evidence only; indirect support is retained without promotion. Hidden and group access remain unresolved. An exclusive adjacent combination is not proof of removal or transformation.

Mandatory edges compose conjunctively, explicit anchor alternatives disjunctively. Contradictions and invalid edges cannot be silently dropped. Exact assistant contacts from P1 constrain the associated route only. Every classical unresolved condition is retained separately. Threat and rescue hypotheses remain separate; no cancellation or final formation/breakage/rescue.

Climate V5 projects exact visible stems, unexposed substantial exact roots, source groups, and unresolved alternatives. Route participants are linked by actual visible occurrence ID. No generic seasonal veto, missing exact stem is not proof of inadequate climate, and no automatic supply/temperature decision. Climate adequacy remains null, so even a supported route hypothesis is not a confirmed effect.
