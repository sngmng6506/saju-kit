Opt-in `analyzePatternOutcomeV4({saju,birthBasis,policy:PATTERN_OUTCOME_V4_VERSION})` / `pattern-outcome-evidence-v4`. The original pattern V1–V10 and outcome V1–V3 outputs remain frozen; no consumer or transit migration.

## Responsibility and conditions

- The existing `strength-decision-v1` owns the day-master directional hypothesis, including grounds and conflicts. V4 projects those fields without overriding the policy or granting authority. It does not evaluate strength for provisional or incomplete charts. Complete charts incur an additional strength-policy computation; no global cache. Strong/weak is an experimental hypothesis, not proof of resource/killing excess.
- V10 relative-force profiles supply occurrence-specific roots, season axes, local generation/control and formation contacts. Boolean evidence dominance is not an effective-force comparison. Shared root positions and repeated controller IDs are reported, not summed. No new weights or thresholds.
- `outcome-regulation.js` enumerates structural candidates for four conditional regulation templates: resource-supported day drained by output; wealth moderating resource; food moderating blade controller; food moderating monthly killing. All participants occupy distinct physical slots. A resource anchor-to-day generation link is required for the output route; no anchor/participant alias multiplication. Hidden anchors remain conditional; actual access unresolved.
- Support-kind is a reference taxonomy for structural probes; the impact explicitly says conditional regulation, not confirmed support. Existing candidate status and assistant list remain unchanged. A controlling edge can support a regulation hypothesis and a damage hypothesis simultaneously. No threat removal, final 成敗, or effective regulation. Excess, deficiency, controller preservation and efficacy remain null; day strong/weak only supplies a supporting/opposing hypothesis where required. Killing deficiency and over-control are unresolved conditions, not assigned numerical limits.
