## Selection authority and conditional anchor relations
`analyzeUsefulElementAuthority({saju,birthBasis,policy:'useful-element-authority-v1'})`.
Composes existing review and expanded climate, retaining exact strength and branch hypotheses. Explicit per-method selectionAuthority:false with reason. For each balance branch, anchors are union of original candidate elements, broad seasonal cue, baseline source stem groups, and robust ordinal mediation candidates; origins preserved, no votes/weights. Unresolved source alternatives excluded. Remedy observations cannot create anchor until desirable target resolved. Conditional relation to each anchor distinguishes same, generates anchor, generated by anchor, controls anchor, controlled by anchor; these are not automatically favorable or unfavorable. All favorable/unfavorable/usefulElement fields null. Opposite tendencies are recorded, not proven conflicts or contraindications. Complete verified chart yields withheld authority; partial/provisional charts do not create branches. Special patterns and method precedence remain unresolved. Caller cannot override direction or selected element.

Actual comparison and authority functions executed on the stored 100-chart reports (not live wrapper; remedy motifs not reconstructed, and cannot select anchors). 150 branches, 449 distinct element hypotheses across branches, 43 branches with opposite tendencies, zero confirmed selections. [Record](useful-element-authority-results.json).

## Temporal evidence
`analyzeUsefulElementTransit({saju,birth,birthBasis,gender,fromYear,toYear,daeunPolicy,policy:'useful-element-transit-v1'})`.
Requires complete verified chart, exact solar birth time, recomputes daeun via explicit legacy-day-v1 or movement-minute-v6 policy (using existing birth/chart consistency). Rejects invalid years, dates before birth year, beyond ten bands and incompatible birth. Partial/provisional/period birth unavailable. Annual pillar after Ipchun; daeun uses existing civil-year bands and counting-age convention. Even minute-based start age is not an exact transition timestamp. Birth year annual label does not imply it applies before birth or Ipchun. Pre-daeun years retain annual-only observation rather than invent a band. For each year:
- visible and hidden stem ten gods and element relations remain distinct;
- natal/transit and daeun/seun stem combinations, full branch relations and clashes are observed; no mutation of natal chart;
- complete distinct-branch groups preserve occurrence IDs, newMembership distinguishes preexisting groups from newly completed groups, requiredTransitLayers checks removal of each layer separately (can be empty if either layer independently suffices);
- every conditional anchor records literal arrivals, elemental relation and exact source-stem matches separately; no auspiciousness or score.

Actual 100 charts × 60 synthetic paired transit pillars (not chronological daeun simulations): 6,000 pairings, 54,000 pair observations, 2,840 complete group observations including 2,300 new group memberships, 580 requiring both layers, 45,000 conditional anchor arrival relations. Checked distinct branches, preexisting groups, no confirmed effects, input immutability and exact stem matches. Separate actual 15-module calendar graph (real bundled calendar data) with three synthetic births × both genders × two policies, 312 year evaluations, 47 pre-daeun annual-only years, 2,573 pair observations. Three of six birth/gender configurations have differing start ages by policy. Birth mismatch rejected. [Calendar record](useful-element-transit-calendar-results.json). These checks exercised actual calendar and pure transit evidence, not the full authority/transit wrapper.
