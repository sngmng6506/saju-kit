## Formation observations (overlapping counts)
31 charts have a clash, 27 have a day-root exposed to a clash, 8 have a complete three-harmony or seasonal-harmony group, 2 of those 8 overlap a clash at a participating position, and 44 have a three-harmony pair candidate including a cardinal branch. Counts are chart-level, not independent path counts. Candidate pairs are not complete groups, complete groups are not confirmed transformations, and clash overlap does not establish cancellation. H01 has an adjacent Shen-Zi water candidate overlapping Zi-Wu clash; no full water group. XU19 has Hai-Mao wood candidates without a clash. Neither case justifies deleting its root.

## Decision
Reject C1/C3 as production changes. C2 has no demonstrated directional benefit. Preserve the current v4/fixed separated policy, no automatic combination or clash effect. Next evaluate a non-destructive formation evidence layer: preserve original element mass, distinguish complete groups from cardinal pairs and other pairs, report position/season/competing relation flags and affected roots, without granting confirmation authority. Freeze its conditions before new source selection. Investigate whether uncertain formation-dependent cases need explicit scope warnings rather than forced ordinary-chart classification. Native replay and full regression not executed here.
